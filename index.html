Dreams of dying young. A “true prophecy” in his gut. Moses. A “time traveler.” Flickering lights. Spiritual war. And, after his death, people closest to him explicitly using the word prophet.

How to use this page: every SHOW button is kept in the same on-air order. “Exact cue” is the transcript-aligned spoken moment. “Pre-roll” opens the same video 10 seconds earlier so you can establish context before the line lands.
